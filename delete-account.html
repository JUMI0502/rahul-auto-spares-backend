Delete Your Account New Rahul Auto Spares — Customer App You can request deletion of your New Rahul Auto Spares account and all associated personal data at any time. How to Request Deletion Send a deletion request using either method below, using the phone number registered on your account: WhatsApp: Message us at the store's WhatsApp number (available in the app under Home → WhatsApp Us) with the text "Delete my account" along with your registered phone number. In-app: Go to Profile → Customer Support and request account deletion, including your registered phone number. What Gets Deleted Your name and phone number Your order history Your loyalty points and rewards balance Your saved bike/vehicle details Your push notification token What May Be Retained We may retain limited transaction records (such as completed order amounts, without your name attached) where required for accounting or legal purposes, even after account deletion. Timeframe Deletion requests are processed within a reasonable timeframe, typically within a few business days.
